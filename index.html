❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about VIATEUR POS PRO.

What is VIATEUR POS PRO?
VIATEUR POS PRO is a comprehensive point-of-sale and business management system designed for small to medium-sized businesses. It includes inventory management, sales processing, barcode scanning, receipt printing, customer management, and real-time analytics.
Do I need any special hardware?
No! VIATEUR POS PRO works on any modern device with a browser. For barcode scanning, you can use a USB barcode scanner or even your phone's camera. Receipt printing works with standard thermal printers.
Can I use it offline?
Yes, the system works offline with local storage. When you reconnect to the internet, data syncs automatically to your account.
Is my data secure?
Absolutely. We use industry-standard encryption for data transmission and storage. Your data is private and only accessible to your authorized users.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?
Yes, you can change your plan at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately, and downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.
